Why we exist

A diagnosis should not depend on what you earn

In Karimnagar, a single CT scan at a commercial centre can cost more than a daily-wage family earns in a fortnight. Too many patients delay a scan, skip a test, or simply go home untreated because of the price on the board.

This centre was set up to remove that barrier. It is run as a service, not as a business:

  • Charges are fixed and displayed publicly on a board at the reception — the same board reproduced on this website.
  • No profit margin is built into any test. Collections go towards running the centre and the Lions Clubs' service work.
  • No commissions are paid to or taken from any doctor, hospital or agent for sending patients here.
  • Every patient is charged the same rate, whoever sends them and whoever they are.

Getting a test done

How it works

No appointment system to navigate and no paperwork beyond your name and contact number.

1. Call or walk in

Ring us on +91 73311 38844 to check what a test costs and what preparation it needs, or simply come to the centre during working hours.

2. Registration & sample

Your details are recorded at the counter and you pay the listed charge. Blood and urine samples are collected at the sample collection point; CT patients are taken to the scan room.

3. Collect your report

Reports are handed over at the centre. Ask the counter when yours will be ready — routine blood work is usually much quicker than specialised profiles.

Tell someone who needs it

The board at our reception ends with a request in Telugu: please make use of this facility, and tell the people you know about it. If a neighbour has been putting off a scan because of the cost, this is what the centre was built for.
