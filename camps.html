Our current programme

SHAKTHI — healthy girls, stronger nation

SHAKTHI is a girls' health screening initiative run as a District Programme for 2026–27 by Lions International District 320G and the Lions District 320G Service Trust, in schools and colleges across the district.

Every girl screened under the programme receives a free Complete Blood Picture (CBP) — a simple blood test, taken on the spot at her own college, that helps identify:

  • Haemoglobin levels and anaemia
  • Red and white blood cell abnormalities
  • Platelet count
  • Other important blood-related abnormalities

Anaemia is widespread among adolescent girls in this country, and it is easy to miss and straightforward to treat once it is found. The point of SHAKTHI is simply to find it — know your blood, know your health.

Beyond SHAKTHI

Other screening our clubs take up

The exact scope of any camp depends on the doctors, equipment and volunteers available on the day, and is announced beforehand in the area where it is being held.

Eye & sight screening

Vision testing and screening for cataract and other common eye conditions — the service Lions Clubs have been associated with worldwide since the organisation took up the cause of blindness prevention.

Diabetes screening

Blood sugar testing and awareness on diet, foot care and the complications of untreated diabetes — one of the global causes Lions Clubs International works on.

General health check-up

Blood pressure, weight, basic clinical examination and consultation with visiting doctors, with referral onward to a hospital where something needs following up.

Blood donation drives

Lions Clubs regularly organise voluntary blood donation camps with local blood banks — a long-standing part of club service work across the district.

Awareness & counselling

Guidance on when a symptom needs investigating, what a prescribed test actually looks for, and where affordable follow-up care can be found locally.

Referral to the centre

Where a camp screening suggests something needs a proper scan or laboratory investigation, patients are guided to our centre at Ashok Nagar, where the subsidised charges apply.
