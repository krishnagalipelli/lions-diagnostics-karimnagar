Finding us

The centre is inside the Indian Red Cross Bhavan at Ashok Nagar, Karimnagar, on the ground floor. The building is directly opposite the Union Bank branch. Once inside, look for the blue and gold Lions emblem above the reception counter — the signage reads Diagnostic Center · CT Scan Center, and the sample collection point is signposted to the right of the counter.

If you cannot find it, call any of the numbers above and someone will direct you.

Medical emergencies: this is a diagnostic centre, not a hospital, and we do not provide emergency or casualty care. In an emergency please go to the nearest hospital or call the national ambulance number 108.

Before you call

Having these ready helps us give you a straight answer in one call:

  • The prescription or the exact name of the test advised
  • The patient's age, and whether she is or may be pregnant if a CT scan is involved
  • Any known allergy to contrast dye, and any kidney condition
  • Earlier reports or scans for the same complaint, if there are any
