About Our Centre

A diagnostic centre run as a public service by Lions Clubs International, District 320G, from the Indian Red Cross Bhavan at Karimnagar.

Who we are

Diagnostics run as a service, not as a business

The Lions CT Scan & Diagnostic Centre is a community service project of Lions Clubs International, District 320G, operating from the ground floor of the Indian Red Cross Bhavan in Ashok Nagar, Karimnagar.

The centre brings together a CT scan facility and a pathology laboratory under one roof and offers both at subsidised charges. The purpose is straightforward: in a district where a great many families live on daily or seasonal earnings, the cost of an investigation should never be the thing that stands between a patient and a diagnosis.

We are not a commercial diagnostic chain. There are no profit targets, no marketing incentives and no referral commissions in either direction. What a test costs is printed on a board at our reception, and that is what you pay.

What guides us

The principles we work by

Transparent pricing

Every charge is on public display at the reception and reproduced in full on this website. There are no package rates, no negotiated rates and no different price for different patients.

Open to everyone

The centre serves the general public of Karimnagar and the surrounding mandals. You do not need to be connected with the Lions Clubs, or with any hospital, to be tested here.

Your information stays yours

Health information is confidential. Reports are released to the patient or to someone the patient authorises, and we do not sell or share patient data. See our privacy policy.

Service beyond the counter

Lions Clubs of District 320G run free screening camps across the district — including SHAKTHI, which gives girls in schools and colleges a free Complete Blood Picture test at their own campus.

Careful reporting

Samples are handled and reported through the centre's laboratory process. We report what the test shows; interpreting it for your case is your treating doctor's work, and we always say so.

No unnecessary waiting

Walk in during working hours, register at the counter and give your sample. There is no appointment system to work around and no queue built by an online booking window.

The wider movement

Lions Clubs International and District 320G

Lions Clubs International is one of the world's largest service club organisations, with member clubs in more than 200 countries and geographic areas. Its motto is simply “We Serve”, and its clubs are known for work in sight and eye health, diabetes awareness, disaster relief, hunger and community health.

District 320G covers clubs in this part of Telangana. Like Lions districts everywhere, its clubs raise funds locally and put them into projects their own community needs — and in Karimnagar, one of those needs was affordable diagnostics. This centre is the result.

The Lions Clubs International Foundation, the charitable arm of the organisation, supports large-scale service projects of this kind undertaken by clubs and districts around the world.

An independent, locally run facility. This website represents the Lions CT Scan & Diagnostic Centre at Karimnagar. Lions Clubs International and its Foundation are separate organisations with their own websites; this centre is operated locally by Lions Clubs of District 320G, and enquiries about it should come to us on the numbers below.
