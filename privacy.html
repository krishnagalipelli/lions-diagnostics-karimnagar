Privacy Policy

How the Lions CT Scan & Diagnostic Centre, Karimnagar collects, uses and protects the information you share with us — at our counter, on this website and over WhatsApp.

Last updated: 19 September 2026

1. Who we are

The Lions CT Scan & Diagnostic Centre (“the Centre”, “we”, “us”) is a not-for-profit diagnostic facility operated as a community service project of Lions Clubs International, District 320G, at the Indian Red Cross Bhavan, Ground Floor, Opposite Union Bank, Ashok Nagar, Karimnagar, Telangana 505001, India.

This policy explains what personal information we handle, why we handle it, and what choices you have. It applies to this website, to messages you send us on WhatsApp or by telephone, and to the records we keep when you have a test done at our Centre.

2. Information we collect

At the Centre. When you register for a scan or a laboratory test we record what we need to carry out and report that test correctly:

  • Your name, age and sex
  • A contact telephone number
  • The test or scan advised, and the referring doctor or hospital where there is one
  • Clinical information relevant to the test — for example pregnancy, allergy to contrast dye, or a kidney condition, all of which affect how a CT scan is done
  • The samples you provide, and the results of the investigations carried out on them
  • The charge paid and the receipt issued

On this website. This website is a plain informational website. It has no login, no account and no form that submits your details to us. We do not run advertising trackers or analytics profiling on it. Two third-party services are loaded to make the site work, and each of them will see your IP address as a normal consequence of your browser requesting a file:

  • Google Fonts — supplies the typeface used on these pages.
  • Google Maps — supplies the embedded map on our Contact page, and is subject to Google's own privacy policy.

Our hosting provider keeps standard server logs, which may include IP address, browser type and the pages requested, for security and reliability purposes.

On WhatsApp and by telephone. If you call us or message us on WhatsApp, we receive your telephone number, your WhatsApp profile name as you have set it, and the content of what you send us — which may include a photograph of a prescription or an earlier report. WhatsApp messages are delivered through WhatsApp's own infrastructure and are also subject to WhatsApp's privacy policy.

3. Why we use your information

  • To carry out the test or scan you have asked for, and to prepare and release your report
  • To identify you correctly and match a sample and a report to the right person
  • To tell you when your report is ready, or to reach you if there is a problem with a sample or an urgent finding that your doctor needs to know about
  • To answer questions you ask us about charges, preparation or timings
  • To maintain the accounts and records the Centre is required to keep
  • To comply with applicable law, and to respond to a lawful order of a court or a competent authority

4. WhatsApp communications

We use WhatsApp to answer enquiries and to communicate with patients who have chosen to contact us on it. The following apply:

  • We message a person on WhatsApp only where that person has contacted us first or has given us their number for this purpose.
  • We use it for service communication — test charges, preparation instructions, timings, report readiness and appointment-related messages, and occasional notice of a free health camp in the area.
  • We do not sell or rent WhatsApp numbers to anyone, and we do not pass them to any third party for marketing.
  • You can stop receiving messages from us at any time by replying STOP to any message, or by telling us on a call. We will act on that request.
  • Please do not send us information about anyone other than yourself, or the patient you are accompanying, without their knowledge.

5. Confidentiality of health information

A diagnostic report is confidential. We release a report to the patient, or to a person the patient has authorised to collect it, or to the doctor or hospital named on the referral. We do not publish, sell, rent or trade patient information, and we do not share it with insurers, employers, marketers or data brokers.

We may disclose information where the law requires it — for example a notifiable disease reportable to the public health authorities, or a lawful direction from a court or a competent government authority.

6. Who else may handle your information

Beyond the Centre's own staff, your information may be handled by:

  • The doctor or hospital that referred you, where a report is sent to them
  • An external reference laboratory, in the limited case where a specialised investigation has to be referred out — we will tell you if that applies to your test
  • Service providers who support our operations, such as our website host and WhatsApp, each only to the extent needed for that service

We require anyone handling information on our behalf to keep it confidential and to use it only for the purpose we gave it to them.

7. How long we keep it

We keep patient registration records, reports and the accounting records relating to them for as long as we need them to serve you and to meet our legal and record-keeping obligations, after which they are disposed of securely. WhatsApp conversations are retained for a reasonable period so that we can follow up on an enquiry, and are then cleared.

8. Security

We take reasonable measures to protect the information in our custody, including restricting access to patient records to the staff who need them and keeping physical records secured at the Centre. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security — but we will act promptly on any incident that affects your information.

9. Your choices and your rights

Subject to applicable Indian law, including the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, you may:

  • Ask us what information we hold about you
  • Ask us to correct information that is wrong or out of date
  • Ask us to erase information we are not required to keep
  • Withdraw consent for us to contact you on WhatsApp
  • Raise a grievance about how we have handled your information

To exercise any of these, call us on +91 73311 38844 or come to the Centre in person. We may need to confirm your identity before releasing or changing a health record, in your own interest.

10. Children

This website is not directed at children. Where a test is carried out on a child, we deal with the parent or guardian accompanying that child, and the report is released to them.

11. Links to other websites

This website may link to other websites, such as those of Lions Clubs International. We do not control those sites and are not responsible for their content or their privacy practices.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The revised version will be posted on this page with a new “last updated” date, and takes effect from the date it is posted.

13. Contact us
