Terms of Service

The terms on which we provide diagnostic services at our Centre and information through this website.

Last updated: 19 September 2026

1. About these terms

These terms apply to the use of this website and to the diagnostic services provided by the Lions CT Scan & Diagnostic Centre at the Indian Red Cross Bhavan, Ashok Nagar, Karimnagar, Telangana, a community service project of Lions Clubs International, District 320G. By using this website or our services, you accept these terms.

2. This website is information, not medical advice

Everything on this website — including descriptions of tests and scans — is general information about the services we offer. It is not medical advice, not a diagnosis and not a substitute for consulting a qualified doctor. Never decide to have, or not to have, an investigation on the strength of this website alone, and never delay seeking medical attention because of something you read here.

3. Emergencies

We are a diagnostic centre. We are not a hospital and we do not provide emergency, casualty or inpatient care. In a medical emergency, go to the nearest hospital or call the national ambulance service on 108. Do not use this website, WhatsApp or our telephone numbers to seek emergency help.

4. Our services

  • Services are provided on a walk-in basis during our working hours. Please call before you travel to confirm the day's timings and availability, particularly for CT scans.
  • Some investigations require preparation, such as fasting. It is important that you follow the preparation advised; a test done without it may have to be repeated, and the charge for the repeat test will apply.
  • We may decline or defer an investigation where, in our judgement, it would not be safe or appropriate to carry it out — for example a CT scan where pregnancy is suspected, or a contrast study where there is a relevant allergy or kidney condition.
  • A CT scan or laboratory test may need to be repeated where a sample is inadequate, where the study is technically unsatisfactory, or where your doctor asks for it.

5. Charges

  • Our charges are displayed on the board at our reception and published on our price list page. They are in Indian Rupees and are payable at the time of registration.
  • The board displayed at our reception is the authoritative statement of the current charge for any test. Where the website and the board differ, the board applies.
  • Charges may be revised from time to time without prior notice on this website.
  • Where a CT study requires contrast, the contrast charge applies in addition to the charge for the body part scanned.
  • We do not pay or accept commissions, referral fees or incentives of any kind, from or to any doctor, hospital, agent or individual, for sending patients to this Centre.

6. Reports

  • Reports are issued on the basis of the sample provided and the clinical information given to us at registration. Incomplete or incorrect information can affect a result.
  • A report states what the investigation found. It is not a diagnosis. Please take your report to the doctor treating you, who will interpret it in the light of your history and examination.
  • Reports are released to the patient, to a person the patient has authorised, or to the referring doctor or hospital. Please bring the receipt issued to you when collecting a report.
  • Laboratory investigations have inherent limits of accuracy and reference ranges vary between methods. Where a result does not fit the clinical picture, your doctor may reasonably ask for it to be repeated or confirmed.

7. Communicating with us on WhatsApp

  • We use WhatsApp to answer enquiries about charges, preparation, timings and reports, and to tell people about free health camps.
  • Messages sent to us over WhatsApp are not a consultation and are not monitored around the clock. Do not use WhatsApp for anything urgent.
  • We will message you only if you have contacted us or have given us your number for the purpose. You may stop messages at any time by replying STOP.
  • How we handle what you send us is set out in our privacy policy.

8. Your responsibilities

  • Give us accurate personal and clinical information at registration — particularly about pregnancy, allergies, current medication and kidney disease.
  • Follow the preparation instructions given to you for your test.
  • Treat our staff and other patients with courtesy. We may refuse service where behaviour puts our staff, our patients or our equipment at risk.
  • Do not use this website for any unlawful purpose, or attempt to interfere with its operation.
