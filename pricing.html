Please note:

• All charges are in Indian Rupees and are payable at our reception counter at the time of registration.

• A CT contrast study is charged in addition to the scan charge for the relevant body part, where the study calls for it.

• Some tests need preparation — fasting for FBS and lipid profile, for instance. Please call before you come so you are not sent back.

• Charges may be revised from time to time. The board displayed at our reception is the final authority on the rate for any test.

• A test result is not a diagnosis. Please take your report back to the doctor treating you.

Not sure which test you need?

Call us with your doctor's prescription in hand and we will tell you what each investigation on it costs here, and what preparation it needs.
